Communication Skills

Communication is one of the most important skills in the tourism industry. Every day tourism professionals interact with customers, suppliers, hotel representatives, transport providers, and international visitors. Strong communication ensures that information is delivered accurately, customers feel valued, and business operations run smoothly.

As an international student studying Information Technology in Australia, I regularly communicate with classmates, lecturers, and people from different cultural backgrounds. This has improved my confidence, listening ability, and verbal communication skills.

Team Communication

Throughout my studies at CQUniversity, I have worked on several group projects where effective communication was essential. I contributed ideas, participated in discussions, and ensured that team members understood their responsibilities. Working collaboratively helped me develop leadership and teamwork skills.

Customer Communication

In the tourism industry, customers often require assistance with travel bookings, accommodation, transportation, and destination information. I understand the importance of communicating clearly, professionally, and respectfully when helping customers.

Good communication creates trust and helps businesses maintain positive relationships with customers. I always aim to listen carefully before providing solutions to ensure customer satisfaction.

Presentation Skills

University presentations have helped me improve my public speaking abilities. I have learned how to organize information effectively, maintain audience engagement, and present confidently. These skills are valuable in both professional and academic environments.

Multicultural Communication

Australia is a multicultural country, and I regularly interact with people from different nationalities and cultural backgrounds. This experience has helped me develop cultural awareness and adapt my communication style according to different situations.

Personal Reflection

Communication is a skill that I continue to improve every day. Through university studies, teamwork, and social interactions, I have become more confident when speaking with others. I believe strong communication skills will help me succeed in the tourism and technology industries in the future.
